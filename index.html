About the Festival

The Dia de Los Deftones Festival takes place in San Diego, CA. The festival has had seven successful years, and each year seems to get better than the last. The festival's name is a playful nod to Día de los Muertos and Deftones' frontman, Chino Moreno, who has Mexican heritage. His father is Mexican, and his mother is part Mexican. The band formed in Sacramento, CA, in 1988 and has released hits such as "Change (In the House of Flies)," "Cherry Waves," and "Passenger." Their music is often described as alternative metal, and although the band formed in the late '80s, they have a huge Gen Z and millennial following. This year's festival sold out, with 40,000 fans attending! The lineup included LustSickPuppy, Lip Critic, Ladrones, Jehnny Beth, Converge, Bktherula, and our favorites, AFI and The Neighbourhood. The festival usually falls on Día de los Muertos weekend, hence the playful name, Dia de Los Deftones. This year, however, the festival was held the following weekend, on Saturday, November 7. The festival featured two stages, one with standing-room viewing and the other with reserved seating. The main stage was where Deftones performed.

The festival had beautiful altares, and the Deftones' ofrenda was the visual centerpiece, paying homage to those who have passed. There were custom sculptures all around, along with beautiful lowriders. Festivalgoers dressed for the theme as Catrinas and Catrines, spending hours creating their looks and weeks planning their outfits.

Festival Highlights

The highlights for us—my little sisters and me—were being able to gather after a very heavy year and watch the bands we listened to growing up. My little sisters are actually my younger cousins, April and Marlene. Marlene is 27, and April is 18. Marlene and I grew up together, and even with a five-year age gap, we loved listening to AFI when we were kids. Their mom, my aunt Gina, introduced us to AFI, and boy, watching them perform 20 years after I first heard their hit, "Miss Murder," was definitely a bit healing. This was April's first "grown-up" experience, and I felt so lucky to have the honor of attending the festival with her. It was a bit of a full-circle moment when The Neighbourhood came onstage. It was Marlene's favorite band when she was a teen and the first band she ever saw live. For her 16th birthday, I surprised her with tickets to see The Neighbourhood at the House of Blues in San Diego, and our other cousin helped arrange a meet-and-greet. These kinds of moments and events are what I look forward to after difficult times, busy schedules, and heartache.

Festival Atmosphere

The atmosphere was great! People were so excited and friendly! I went with a cute '90s alt look, and I kept getting compliments on my skirt. The crowds weren't as hectic as I thought, but man, the energy when we rushed to get a good spot for AFI's performance was so high! People were respectful and kept their distance. I thought we'd deal with some shoving and elbowing, but no! We were able to jump up and down, move side to side, record, and have a blast. Everyone around us was a little older and was enjoying the nostalgia of listening to one of their favorite bands growing up.

Live Performances

The energy was magnetic! We were smiling from ear to ear even as we scurried through crowds. I would look back and see my little sisters following behind me. People were shouting out lyrics at the top of their lungs and then rushing over to the next stage for the next performance! That was a fun little rush. Thankfully, we had reserved seating for the main stage, while the second stage was standing-room only.

Takeaways

Now that we've attended a Dia de Los Deftones festival, we know what to expect for next year's event. We know that Petco Park concessions do not accept cash and can be quite pricey. We should definitely head to Petco Park the day before to check out the merchandise. That way, the crowds aren't as big, and it's more likely that better sizes will be available. We will make sure to each bring a phone charger or bring at least one portable charger with enough ports for all our phones. And lastly, we'll buy our tickets in advance to avoid paying reseller prices! The tickets were a gift, but my uncle paid quite a bit for them.
